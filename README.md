# Exam Countdown Tracker

A static, mobile-first exam countdown dashboard designed for GitHub Pages.

## Included milestones

1. UIIC AO 2026 — Preliminary — 22 Oct 2026 (tentative)
2. IBPS RRB Officer Scale I XV — Preliminary — 21 Nov 2026
3. UIIC AO 2026 — Main — 30 Nov 2026 (tentative)
4. IBPS RRB Office Assistant XV — Preliminary — 6 Dec 2026
5. IBPS RRB Officer Scale I XV — Main — 20 Dec 2026
6. IBPS RRB Office Assistant XV — Main — 30 Jan 2027
7. GATE 2027 — Computer Science — target 7 Feb 2027
8. Remaining days in 2026 — calculated live

## GitHub Pages setup

1. Create a new GitHub repository, for example `exam-countdown`.
2. Upload `index.html`, `styles.css`, and `script.js`.
3. Go to Settings → Pages.
4. Under Build and deployment, choose `Deploy from a branch`.
5. Select the `main` branch and `/root` folder.
6. Save.
7. GitHub will provide the Pages URL.
8. Open that URL on your phone and add it to your Home Screen.

## Updating an exam date

Open `script.js` and edit the `exams` array. The countdown automatically recalculates; no server/database is required.

## Important date note

The uploaded PDFs are application/reprint forms. They identify the exams/posts but do not contain the actual exam schedule. The dates used in this tracker were therefore filled from the published 2026–27 schedules.

UIIC AO dates are tentative and should be checked against the official call letter.

GATE 2027 officially runs across Feb 6, 7, 13, 14, 20 and 21, 2027. The CS paper target in this tracker is Feb 7; confirm the final paper/session from the GATE admit card.
